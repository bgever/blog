Hi, I’m Bart Verkoeijen — a software engineer and entrepreneur from the Netherlands, living in
Hong Kong.

I build for the web: full-stack applications, mobile apps, and the cloud infrastructure underneath
them. Most of my working time goes into [Flippercast](https://flippercast.com), the software
consultancy and SaaS company I founded, where I care as much about how something feels to use as
whether it holds up under load.

This blog is where I write about the craft — what I’m learning, what I got wrong, and the occasional
thing worth remembering. It’s an extension of my CV as much as it is a notebook.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/bgever) and
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bartverkoeijen/), or reach me through
[Flippercast](https://flippercast.com).
